# Watching Football
## Passionate Support & Statistical Analysis

**Badge**: FOOTBALL FAN
**Tagline**: Tracking the stats while surviving the emotional rollercoaster of being a Spurs fan.

### Summary
Johan Sofalas brings a unique blend of analytical rigor and unwavering loyalty to his football fandom. He proudly supports Tottenham Hotspur and the Sri Lankan national team—proving his dedication to the beautiful game's emotional extremes, from top-flight Premier League drama to the ultimate international underdog story. While he firmly respects the undeniable dual-GOAT status of both Messi and Ronaldo, his all-time favorite player remains Philippe Coutinho, with a special appreciation for those signature outside-the-box curlers. For Johan, the joy of football lies in the contrast: logically analyzing statistical matchups and expected goals (xG), while still passionately screaming at the television during the 90th-minute in a Spurs game.
