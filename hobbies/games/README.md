# Tabletop & Party Games
## Social Deduction & Group Strategy

**Badge**: BOARD GAMER
**Tagline**: Navigating deception, strategy, and sheer chaos.

### Summary
Johan Sofalas is an avid player of tabletop and party games, thriving in the highly interactive environments of social deduction titles like *Blood on the Clocktower*, *The Resistance*, *Two Rooms and a Boom*, and *Mafia*, while also enjoying classics like *Scrabble*. For him, the appeal lies in a dynamic combination of psychological bluffing, asymmetric roles, and the unpredictable chaos of group dynamics.

### Specs
- GENRE: Social Deduction & Classic
- FOCUS: Group Dynamics
- PLAYSTYLE: Competitive & Analytical
- VIBE: High Interaction
