# Watching Cricket
## Analytical Fandom & Lifelong Support

**Badge**: CRICKET FAN
**Tagline**: Cheering the game while tracking the stats.

### Summary
Johan Sofalas is a lifelong cricket fan who supports the Sri Lankan national team and the Delhi Capitals across all three formats (Test, ODI, T20). While his primary focus is on enjoying the flow of the game and cheering on his teams, his analytical background naturally draws him to the statistical side of the sport—closely tracking matchups, correlations, and causations that dictate play. He holds strong personal opinions on the game's greatest talents, carefully evaluating favorite players across batting (Mahela Jayawardene), bowling (Nuwan Kulasekara), and all-round capabilities (Angelo Mathews, with Jacques Kallis as the greatest of all time), alongside leadership (Best Captain: Mahela Jayawardene). He also maintains that T20 cricket is becoming too batter-friendly, views the "Test is the only real format" crowd as overly nostalgic, and strongly believes ODIs deserve equal respect.
