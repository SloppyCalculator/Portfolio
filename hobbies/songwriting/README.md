# Creative Writing & Poetry
## Reflective Poetry & Personal Narratives

**Badge**: WRITER
**Tagline**: Exploring the depths of imagination and raw emotion.

### Summary
Johan Sofalas channels his creative energy into authoring narrative fiction, including his YA cyber-thriller *Alphas - The Beginning*, and composing reflective poetry. He approaches storytelling as the attempt to capture vivid dreams on paper, while utilizing poetry as a raw, honest medium to process inner thoughts and personal faith.

- MEDIUM: Poetry & Fiction
- FOCUS: Mystery & Real-Life
- CORE THEME: Christian Faith
- STYLE: Narrative Tribute

- Anchors creative works in real-life emotions and meaningful tributes.
- Balances high-stakes narrative structures with character-first plotting.
