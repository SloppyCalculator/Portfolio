# Reading Storybooks
## Mystery, Adventure & The Classics

**Badge**: READER
**Tagline**: Immersed in classic mysteries, deep character arcs, and driven plots.

### Summary
Johan Sofalas is an avid reader with a strong affinity for mystery, adventure, and romance. A huge fan of classic sleuthing tales—particularly the *Hardy Boys*, *Nancy Drew*, and their crossover *Supermysteries*—he appreciates the nostalgic thrill of deduction and teamwork. Beyond these mysteries, Johan enjoys diving into the classics of literature. Above all, a book must feature a genuine, well-structured plot and deep character development to capture his attention, ensuring the narrative is both structurally sound and emotionally engaging.

### Specs
- GENRES: Mystery, Adventure, Romance
- FAVORITES: Hardy Boys, Nancy Drew
- PREFERENCE: The Classics
- FOCUS: Plot & Character Depth

### Highlights
- Gravitates toward the nostalgic sleuthing of *Hardy Boys* and *Nancy Drew* mysteries.
- Requires strong, actual plotlines paired with deep, immersive character development.
- Enjoys the timeless narratives and foundational storytelling found within classic literature.
