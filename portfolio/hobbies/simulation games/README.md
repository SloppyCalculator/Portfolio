# Sports Management Simulators
## Youth Academies & Lower-League Rebuilds

**Badge**: VIRTUAL GAFFER
**Tagline**: Scouting the wonderkids, rebuilding the giants, and tinkering with the tactics.

### Summary
In the realm of sports management simulators like *Football Manager 26*, *Cricket Captain 26*, and *Tennis Manager 2026*, Johan Sofalas operates as a youth academy purist with a deep appreciation for the long game. Rather than buying ready-made superstars, he spends his time meticulously diving into databases and scouting networks to unearth cheap, high-potential wonderkids based on raw stats. His managerial style balances this long-term, data-driven recruitment with occasional tactical tinkering on match days. Johan's ultimate endgame is always a classic zero-to-hero narrative: taking a lower-league underdog to the absolute top of the pyramid, or stepping in to rebuild a fallen giant back to its former glory.

### Specs
- GAMES: FM26, Cricket Captain 26, TM 2026
- STYLE: Youth Academy Purist
- FOCUS: Wonderkid Scouting & Stats
- ENDGAME: Lower-League Underdogs

### Highlights
- Prioritizes long-term youth development and academy growth over high-profile transfers.
- Extensively scouts databases to identify cheap wonderkids using statistical analysis.
- Specializes in grueling lower-league saves and rebuilding fallen sporting giants.
