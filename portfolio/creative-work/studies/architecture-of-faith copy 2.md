# Peter

Name - Simon John 21:15-17
Father - Jonah/John Matthew 16:17, John 1:42
Hometown - Bethsaida
Nickname- Cephas - John 1:42
Occupation - Fishing - Matthew 4:18-22
Marital Status - Married - 1 Corinthians 9:5, Matthew 8:14-15
Martyrdom - 64-68 AD by King Nero crucified upside down at the cross
Peter knew what was happening before meeting Christ - John 1:41 - Andrew states that they met the Messiah and that is all. Peter knew who the Messiah was. Andrew his brother was already following John, the man who showed the way for Jesus. So they were on the lookout for the Messiah. They knew about the prophecies, even after the 400 dark years.  Are we knowledgeable on the scriptures. This was before either of them met Jesus. How many of us have read the scriptures? Do we have any idea of the prophecies the future of our world? Or do we have the sufficient knowledge to identify between false preachers and the genuine preachers.2 Timothy 4:3-4 states that people will preach in order to suit their own desires and that they will go towards myths. When this happens do we have the knowledge in order to identify what is false

Peter immediately heeded to Jesus’ call -  There was no hesitation. When Jesus called he immediately left his boat, his fishing net to follow Jesus. At the same time he knew who was calling him. What are our priorities? Luke 9:62. Jesus says one who puts his hand on the plow and looks back isn’t fit to be in the kingdom of God. Peter doesn’t look back he immediately follows Jesus without a doubt. Do we second guess the choices we make for Christ or do we confidently follow behind him. There was no second guessing for Peter. His priority was for Jesus. He completely left what he was doing. All what we do Jesus should be our priority Matthew 6:33. At the same time Peter knew what he was called to do and who was calling him. Even though we get called to do some stuff we should see who is calling us, what are we being called for and whether what we are about to do going to positively impact the kingdom of God.

Peter knew his situation - Luke 5:8
Peter wasn’t going to justify himself , the miracles he got in his life. Once seeing Jesus’ miracle Peter falls on his knees. Many times in our lives we act as if we are entitled to miracles and that is why when things don’t go our way we start blaming God. Peter knew his situation, his life immediately after that miracle and at the same time he notices the purity of Jesus. We see similar instance from Isaiah in Isaiah 6. Both instances God comforts them. When we go into God’s presence are we convicted or do we go for the sake of going and coming. Peter had served Jesus for some time but he gets the comfort when he realizes who he really is.

Peter had faith - Peter knew Jesus for who he truly was Matthew 14:28 - Lord if it is you. The only condition Peter wanted was for it to be Jesus. He didn’t care about anything else at that time. In our journey who is our focus. Are we looking around everywhere or are we telling Jesus if it is you I am ready to come. We look at the situations around us and don’t take the step forward. We ignore the hand raised in front to carry us through. That is why Jesus says if you have the faith of a mustard seed you can move mountains. Peter had that faith. This same Peter openly confessed John 6:68-69, Matthew 16:13-20. He had the spirit of God working in him and at the same time he had that faith. We hear the saying blind leap of faith but or leap isn’t blind. We know that Jesus is there to take us through Hebrews 11:1. Peter didn’t think about the depth of the ocean, he didn’t think about the wind at the beginning. All he cared was Lord if it is you.

Peter was distracted - 
The same guy who took a leap of faith crashes down Matthew 14:29. At the beginning his sight was Lord. And he walked towards Jesus but then he looks around and sees the terrors. He sees the waves the wind and he drowns. Many of us like Peter take a leap of faith. We walk towards Christ trusting in Christ but a small thing makes us get distracted. We lose our focus, our faith and ourselves. The same Peter boldly tells Jesus in the last supper, Lord I am willing to die for you. But at the same time when Jesus tells them to stay awake at the Garden, Peter falls asleep. Matthew 26:36-46. Peter had the zeal the love the faith but at the same time he was distracted. That is why Jesus tells him Matthew 26:41. Is our flesh influencing us. It could be taking a peep into the mobile during service our thoughts wandering all over during a sermon, sleeping while praying. Are we distracting ourselves because of our flesh? Our spirit could be keen to do service for Jesus. We could confess have faith, but is our flesh controlling us. 

Peter was overzealous-
John 18:10-11 - Peter was overzealous. There is a difference between defending Jesus and fighting for Jesus. Peter at this moment goes for war. He goes and cuts the ear of the servant. The main priority we forget is that Christianity’s basis is love 1 Corinthians 13. Yes we must defend Christianity. That is why Elijah goes in front and defends. It is our duty to defend false teachings, misinterpretations of Christ. God is going to ask an account of us if we let people mock him and discriminate him. But at the same time it should be done by love. Not going and ripping someone’s ear. 

He wanted to submit himself completely to God - John 13:9 - Peter cared about God so much, that he wanted to be completely cleansed because Jesus said that if I don’t wash your feet you don’t belong to me. Are we willing to offer ourselves completely to Christ. Or are we halfway doing this and halfway doing something else. Are we not completely submitting ourselves to Christ?
