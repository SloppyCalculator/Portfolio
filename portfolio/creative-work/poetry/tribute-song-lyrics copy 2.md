# Things will never be the same again
## Song
**Lyricist**: Johan Sofalas  

Verse 1
In the car, the brakes gave way
On the tracks, the train lost its way
When the news came through that day
I knew life had changed, forever, not halfway

Verse 2
The calls that used to fill my phone fell still
The laughs we shared now echo, lost and chill
The seat where wisdom used to stay
Sits silent now, it’s never felt so far away

Chorus
Things will never be the same again
Since you’ve gone, I’ve felt the end begin
The echoes of your voice remain
But nothing can return me to back then

Verse 3
You made sure I faced my wrongs with grace
Taught me to hold the good and not erase
Now your lessons light my path ahead
But your absence speaks the loudest in my head

Bridge
When you said those words that day
I wanted so badly to say them your way
I whispered soft beneath my breath
Wishing I’d said it louder, with no regret

Dad... I am so proud of you.

Final Chorus
In your life, the times would change
But your purpose stood through joy and pain
You chased God’s light with steady grace
And lived a life that touched so many face to face
I only pray I walk that way
So when I'm gone, someone might say:
"He lived his life with God above
A heart full of light, a life full of love."
