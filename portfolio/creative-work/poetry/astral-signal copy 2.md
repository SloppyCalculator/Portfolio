# Broken
## Poetry

I guess the alarm never rang that day
The voice which always woke me up withered away
Everything around me were in darkness etched
The birds’ songs to me were just laments

The world just seemed to crumble around me
Without you in my life where would I be
You were my father, my guide, my counselor, my fan, my friend 
I always thought you would be there with me until the end

The evenings filled with guitar and songs are now just silent
The jokes, the laughter, the silliness , the games are now absent
A day doesn’t go by where I don’t remember your smiling face 
Your advice always ring in my ear when I am in a tough phase

Sometimes i just want to sit on the floor and weep
The scar just has struck so deep
The world has seemed to move on
And so must I but my wheels are stuck my drive is gone

But I guess I cannot last this way 
At worse circumstances, you never did sway
To stand firm in my faith and to seek God 
That was the path on which you did trod
