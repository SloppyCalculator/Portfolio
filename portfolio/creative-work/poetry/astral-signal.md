# Faith that Works
## Poetry

One who doesn’t know God came to me and said, 
"The God you worship, the Bible you read, everything is fake. 
You’ll live a life simply saying to me, 'on the cross everything is paid.'"
 Not for what they did expect, but that statement made me take a double take.

Am a living a life so that they could all see the Holy Spirit in me? 
Or is it a show, and am I living a life to sate all my worldly fantasies? 
Cause if i say i believe in Christ, but my life happy in sin be, 
Then my faith is fake, cause how is it real after knowing his miseries?

One who claims to know God sat up on stage, as the interviewer queried, 
‘To see heaven must you believe in Christ or is there another way?’ 
‘I don’t know,’ he said. ‘Cause if unbelievers do good, to heaven they should be carried.’ 
What he said gave me a heartache, cause how many are led astray.

I am born of sin, and nothing I do to heaven could get me. 
I try to do good, but what I don’t like to, do I daily do. 
So God loved me so much, that he sent his Son so that me he could free. 
When I believe, I do not perish, but to eternity he carries me through.

I am saved by grace through faith, and pride in my salvation can’t I. 
Cause what I do in my life can never get me to those beautiful pearly gates. 
But if I do believe and I am saved, the spirit’s fruits in me I can’t deny. 
And my life should be filled with good deeds, with love as the force that dominates.
