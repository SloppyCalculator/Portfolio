# What would I say
## Poetry

What would I say to my younger self? 
What would he think if he saw me right now? 
Where would I measure upon his scale? 
Would where I am cause a crease on his brow?

Would I advise him from all my mistakes, 
Or speak of the blessings I hold in my hands? 
Would I sigh for the lost dreams of that sweet little boy, 
Or the peaks where he never imagined he'd stand?

Would I tell of the darkest paths I had to walk, 
Or the quiet lessons those shadows taught me? 
Would I speak of the thorns I stumbled upon, 
Or the gentle hand guiding the bruised me?

I think I would tell him to savor his youth, 
For I know back then I wished to grow fast. 
To hold every innocent memory close, And revel in freedoms that never quite last.

I would tell him to linger with those that he loves, 
For the shadow of loss takes the dear ones away. 
To soak in their presence and hold no regrets, 
To treasure the ones who were not meant to stay.

And most of all, love the One who remains, 
Who, knowing my flaws, stood firm by my side. 
Who, perfect in image, died for broken me, 
And offered me strength when I had none to fight.

So, would I tell my younger self I carry regret? 
I think not, for the journey has built me, by and by. 
The missed chances forged me, the victories lead; 
Why should I regret, when the One who loves me is writing my story?
