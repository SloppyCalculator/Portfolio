# Weaknesses & Areas for Growth

- **Perfectionism**: A tendency to over-engineer edge cases in low-stakes deployments. Actively practicing rapid MVP scoping to ship high-impact iterations faster.
- **Context Switching Overhead**: Transitioning between highly creative narrative writing and strict mathematical algorithm engineering requires intentional cognitive recalibration.
- **Deep-Dive Rabbit Holes**: Natural curiosity sometimes leads to investigating obscure compiler optimizations or linguistics edge cases beyond immediate sprint requirements.
