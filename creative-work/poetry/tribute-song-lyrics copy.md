# Illusions
## Poetry

---

The light streaming in did my head right in
I wanted to strangle the alarm in my phone blaring
The illusions of my dreams had to be reset
Disappear did the beautiful scenes I had set

The boy proudly up the stage walking in
The dad in the front row happily cheering
“I knew you could do it, you are going to keep on succeeding”
Oh boy, I wish I could keep on dreaming

The father advising the man on his wedding day
Happy to see the woman whom with his son would stay
And his eyes would fill with tears of glee
Oh how I wish this would have been reality

The father watching his son’s kids buzzing like bees
Sneaking them some sweets teaching bible stories
The kids getting to see how cool their grandfather was
But now they’d only hear it through the world’s applause

But then a scene in my dream did appear
Of a broken world filled with drear
The man, a warrior trying to break through the line
Trying to fight against God’s scheduled time

Memories of the past did rush through
All of the things the son with the dad did do
Cherish the memories instead trying to through the fabric tear
The achievements he saw was enough to with a kiss say “I am proud of you my dear”
