# Why human?
## Poetry

Why is there pain?
Why does grief even exist?
Why is there stress?
Why I am so susceptible to hurt?

I wish I could be a robot
Be a pile of nuts and bolts
The only signals passing
Are of the electronic type

Why do I love?
Why do I have to be happy?
Why do I need connection?
What is the purpose of purpose?

Not a fan of being human
It is too complicated
Trying to map all the gates
I seem to running around in circles

Why am I fragile?
Why does someone hurting, hurt me?
Why am I crying?
Looking at a failed relationship

I don’t mind being human
At least there are others like me
I can choose the gates to go
Unlike a robot, I got my own free will

Why was I created?
Why in his own perfect image?
Why did he leave it all?
Why did he die for worthless me?

I love being human
I love being created in his image
I love being able to love
To share it with the other creations of his
