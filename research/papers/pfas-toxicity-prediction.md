**Author**: Johan Sofalas  
**Venue**: Work in Progress  
**Keywords**: PFAS, SMILES Representation, Attention Mechanisms, QSAR  

---

## Abstract
Per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances (PFAS) pose severe ecological and human health hazards due to their environmental persistence ("forever chemicals"). Classical quantitative structure-activity relationship (QSAR) models struggle to capture conformational nuances across diverse fluorinated carbon chains.
