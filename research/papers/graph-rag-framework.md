# Beyond Literalism: A Graph-RAG Architecture for the Semantic Translation of Sinhala Figures of Speech

**Authors**: Johan Sofalas, et al.  
**Venue**: NA  
**Topics**: Knowledge Graphs, Machine Translation, Figures of Speech  

---

## Abstract
Figures of Speech are single or compound word structures which are often heavily tied with cultural nuances which makes translation quite difficult.

We present a multi-tier architecture which can identify, reason and translate figures of speech from Sinhala to English.
